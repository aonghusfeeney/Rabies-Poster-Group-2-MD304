100% Preventable, 100% Fatal

Barriers to rabies elimination on the road to “Zero by 30”

Aonghus Feeney, Omer Faraz, Declan Gbinigie, Yahya Ghanem, Niamh Lernihan, Aoife Leyden, Aoibhin Marren, Sanjana Mehra, Ava Reddington, Raj Tripathi
School of Medicine, University of Galway

Rabies kills an estimated 59,000 people a year. It has been preventable for over a century. This poster asks why that is still true — and finds three failures of delivery, not one failure of science.

25× more deaths are modelled than are ever counted We cannot count it 40.5% of dog-bite victims complete a course of prophylaxis People cannot reach it 1.5% of rabies spending goes to the only intervention that stops transmission We fund the consequence
